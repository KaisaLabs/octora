# Atomic compound ix: pool.claim_fee → mixer.deposit with floor-round

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Type:** enhancement
**Slice:** AFK

## What to build

The fee-claim equivalent of the Position-Open compound ix. Same atomicity principle, different direction: when an LP **Position** earns fees, claim them and recycle them back into the mixer in one transaction.

```
pool.claim_fee -> mixer.deposit (in one tx)
```

Rules:

- Round-**down** claimed fees to the nearest denomination multiple (e.g. floor to 0.1 SOL on the smallest tier)
- Floor portion → fresh **Commitment** on the Mixer Pool (a new Anonymity Set entry)
- **Residual sub-denom dust → compounded back into the DLMM Position** itself. It stays inside the user's own LP, growing the next claim.
- No off-chain sweeper, no protocol treasury siphon, no dust trail.

Constraint: dust returning to the user's own LP must never leave the position account between claim and re-deposit — otherwise it lands in the Stealth Wallet's free balance and creates the exact dust state we're avoiding. Verify by chain trace, not just by balance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On-chain ix chains DLMM `claim_fee` → mixer `deposit` in one tx
- [ ] Floor-rounding to nearest denom multiple is enforced on-chain (off-chain pre-computation is OK as a helper, but the program rejects mismatched amounts)
- [ ] Residual sub-denom remains inside the DLMM Position — verify by inspecting bin balances before/after, not by stealth balance
- [ ] Fresh Commitment lands in the Mixer Pool's Merkle Tree, observable via `DepositEvent`
- [ ] Backend claim path in `octora-api/src/modules/positions/` or `executor/` is updated to call the compound ix
- [ ] Test covers: claim exactly at denom multiple (no residual), claim slightly below denom (no new commitment, all goes back to LP), claim slightly above denom (one commitment + residual back to LP)
